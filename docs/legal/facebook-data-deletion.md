# Yance Facebook Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 2026-09-29

You may request deletion of Facebook Page / Messenger data controlled by Yance.

## How to request deletion
Send an email to berthacqe@hotmail.com with the subject `Yance Facebook Data Deletion Request`.

Include enough information to identify the relevant Yance/Facebook Page connection, such as the Page name or Page ID and the Facebook account or Messenger conversation involved. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other secrets.

## Verification
Yance may ask for reasonable information needed to verify that the requester is entitled to make the request for the relevant Page or conversation.

## What happens after verification
Yance will delete or de-identify data under Yance's control that is covered by the verified request, subject to applicable legal, security, and operational retention requirements. The associated Yance connection can also be disconnected when requested.

Data held independently by Meta/Facebook or other service providers remains subject to those providers' own retention and deletion processes.

## Revoke Meta access
You may also revoke Yance's Facebook permissions through Meta's settings for business integrations or connected apps. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not by itself replace a deletion request for data already stored by Yance.

## Contact
Deletion and privacy requests: berthacqe@hotmail.com
