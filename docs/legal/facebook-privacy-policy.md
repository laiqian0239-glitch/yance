# Yance Privacy Policy for Facebook Page / Messenger

Effective date: 2026-09-29

Yance helps an authorized Facebook Page administrator receive, view, and reply to Page Messenger conversations through the Yance desktop experience.

## Data we process
For an authorized Facebook Page connection, Yance may process Page identifiers, Messenger conversation and message content, attachments, delivery/read metadata, and sender profile fields that Meta makes available for the Page, such as name, profile picture, and platform-scoped identifiers. Yance also processes the minimum authorization and connection metadata needed to operate the integration.

## How we use data
We use this data only to provide the requested messaging experience: synchronize authorized Page conversations, display contacts and messages, send replies, maintain the connection between the authorized Page and the messaging stack, and diagnose integration failures.

## Service providers and infrastructure
The integration relies on Meta/Facebook, Chatwoot, Matrix/Synapse, Element, and infrastructure used to operate Yance. These systems may process data as needed to deliver the service. Yance does not use this Facebook integration to sell personal data.

## Retention and deletion
Data is retained only as needed to provide the messaging service, maintain conversation history requested by the authorized Page administrator, meet security or legal obligations, and resolve operational issues. A user or Page administrator may request deletion using the contact information below.

## Security
Yance uses access controls and platform authorization mechanisms intended to limit processing to authorized Page connections. No Internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and requests
You may revoke Yance's Facebook permissions from Meta and may request access, correction, or deletion of data controlled by Yance. See the Yance Facebook Data Deletion Instructions for the deletion process.
